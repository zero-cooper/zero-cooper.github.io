Bio

{% include icon-links.html %}

I am a final-year Ph.D. researcher in the School of Computer Science at the University of Birmingham (UoB), supervised by Dr. Leonardo Stella @ Dynamic Learning and Control (DLC) group and co-supervised by Prof. Chris Baber @ Multi-Agent Systems (MAS@B) group. I also lead an industrial collaboration with SAIC Mobility Ltd. on scalable taxi operations for smart cities.

Before joining UoB, I worked in the Intelligent Systems Lab, led by Dr. Wen-Chi Yang, at Neuhelium Co., Ltd. in Shanghai from 2020 to 2024. My work combined research and engineering in swarm intelligence and bio-inspired learning algorithms, including spiking neural networks. I received my MSc in Artificial Intelligence from the School of Informatics at the University of Edinburgh in 2019, with a focus on machine learning, probabilistic modelling, and game theory. In 2014, I was direct-admitted to ChangKong College at Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics, where I received my bachelor's degree in Automation Engineering in 2018.
